## 3) Plan Naming Guidance

- Keep names clear and hierarchical: `Free` -> `Starter` -> `Pro` -> `Studio`.
- Use action-oriented internal labels in admin docs:
  - Free: exploration
  - Starter: early paid creator
  - Pro: regular creator workflow
  - Studio: power user/team workflow
- Current app implementation surfaces `Free`, `Pro`, and `Studio`. If `Starter` is introduced, position it as the bridge between trial use and full production cadence.

## 7) FAQ Suggestions

## How do credits work?
Credits are consumed when you run AI generations. Each plan includes a monthly credit allocation, and usage resets each billing cycle.

## What happens when credits run out?
You can wait for the next monthly reset or upgrade to a higher plan for more capacity.

## Can I reuse outputs commercially?
Add a policy section in legal docs tailored to your licensing model. Suggested default copy: outputs can be reused in your projects subject to MusicForge Terms and any third-party model terms.

## Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes. Users can manage cancellations through the billing portal. Access remains consistent with plan status and billing cycle timing.

## When do billing changes take effect?
Upgrades generally apply immediately. Downgrades and cancellations follow the Stripe subscription lifecycle configured for your project.

## 8) Copy Guardrails

- Keep language specific to creator outcomes, not generic AI hype.
- Mention credits and reset behavior clearly to reduce support load.
- Avoid promises not present in the product (for example, instant mastering, guaranteed chart-ready output).
- Keep every pricing line editable and tied to a real capability already implemented.
